Estate services · Naples, Florida

When a home full of history needs a plan.

We help families, executors and trustees across Naples and Southwest Florida sort, value and place the contents of an estate — honestly, discreetly, and without pressure.

First, the thing nobody says out loud

Most people who call us have never done this before. A parent has passed, or a grandparent, or a friend who named you in the will — and now there is a house in Naples with forty years of furniture, silver, paintings and paperwork in it, and a closing date. You are grieving and you are also, suddenly, a logistics manager.

You do not need to know what anything is worth before you call. You do not need to sort it first. You do not need to apologize for the state of the garage. That is the job, and it is the part we are good at.

Who we help

  • Families after a loss — children and grandchildren who want the contents handled respectfully, and want to know what is worth keeping.
  • Executors and personal representatives — who need a defensible, documented process and a clear timeline for the estate's tangible property.
  • Trustees and attorneys — who need one reliable contact, written summaries, and no drama.
  • Realtors — with a listing that has to be emptied, staged, or closed on a date that isn't moving.
  • Out-of-state heirs — who can't be here. We become your eyes on the ground: photographs, a written run-through of what's there, and a plan you can approve from a thousand miles away.

What we actually do

We come to the house — Naples, Marco Island, Bonita Springs, Estero, Fort Myers, or wherever the estate is — and walk through it with you, or with whoever holds the keys. Then we sort the contents into honest categories:

  • Pieces we'd buy outright, with a written offer for each, or for the lot.
  • Pieces that belong at auction — the rare, the signed, the things with a story collectors will pay for. We place these with the right specialist sale and manage the whole process. More on auction placement.
  • Pieces worth consigning through our shop, if you'd rather wait for the right buyer than take a quick number.
  • Things that should stay in the family — we'll tell you when something is worth more to you than to any market.
  • Everything else — donation guidance and, when you need it, a referral for a full clean-out so the house is ready to hand over.

You get all of that in writing. Then you decide. Nothing leaves the house until you say so. For a plain comparison of the four routes — buyout, consignment, auction and estate sale — see estate liquidation, explained.

What we look for

Antique and vintage furniture, sterling silver (tea services, flatware sets, hollowware, candlesticks — monograms are fine), oil paintings, prints, gilt frames and mirrors, porcelain and glass, lighting, clocks, rugs, books, decorative objects, and the odd beautiful thing you'd never guess had value. Most Naples estates are a mix of very good and perfectly ordinary; sorting one from the other is the whole point of a walk-through.

Jewelry, watches, gold, coins and diamonds are handled by our sister shop at the same address, Naples Estate Jewelry. One visit covers both.

Naples realities we plan around

Seasonal residents who are only here from January to April. Condo buildings with elevator reservations, move-out windows and loading-dock rules. Gated communities that need names at the gate. Closings that are three weeks away. Siblings in three time zones. We have worked around all of it, and we would rather hear the complication up front than discover it on moving day.

What we won't do

We won't pressure you. We won't make a low offer and hope you don't check. We won't quietly buy something for a hundred dollars that we know should go to auction for ten thousand. Our business runs on the next phone call from the family down the street, and that only happens if the last one went well.

How it works

One call, then a plan.

Four steps, usually inside two weeks. Faster when a closing demands it.

  1. 01

    Call or write

    Tell us where the house is, roughly what's in it, and any dates that matter. Photos help but aren't required.

  2. 02

    The walk-through

    We come to the estate, free of charge, and go room by room. Out of state? We can work from photographs or with whoever holds the keys.

  3. 03

    Written evaluation

    What's there, what it's likely worth, and your options for each piece — buyout, consignment, auction, keep, or donate.

  4. 04

    We handle it

    Pickup, paperwork, auction submissions, shipping and payment. You get a record of everything for the estate file.

Questions families ask

Is there a fee for the walk-through or the evaluation?

No. The visit and the written evaluation are free, with no obligation. If you sell to us, that's the transaction. If we place pieces at auction or on consignment, the terms are agreed in writing before anything moves.

How quickly can you come out?

Usually within a few days for Naples and Marco Island, and within a week for Bonita Springs, Estero, Fort Myers and Cape Coral. If there's a closing or a move-out deadline, tell us and we'll work to it.

Do you buy everything in the house?

We buy what we can sell well — antiques, silver, art, porcelain, good furniture and decorative objects. For the rest, we'll tell you honestly whether it's worth consigning, donating or clearing, and we can refer a clean-out service if the house needs to be emptied completely.

What about jewelry, gold, watches and coins?

Those go to our sister shop, Naples Estate Jewelry, which shares our address and phone number. One visit can cover the whole estate.

I'm the executor and I live out of state. Can you still help?

Yes — a good share of the estates we handle have heirs in the Northeast or Midwest. We can coordinate access with a neighbor, realtor or attorney, send photographs and a written summary, and handle everything from there with your approval by phone or email.

Do I need a certified appraisal for probate?

Sometimes. Florida probate, estate-tax filings, insurance claims and charitable donations may require a formal appraisal from a credentialed appraiser. Our evaluation is a market opinion, not a certified appraisal; we'll tell you when you need the formal kind and can refer you to an independent appraiser. More on evaluations vs. appraisals.

Do you run estate sales in the house?

We don't run public tag sales. We buy, consign and place at auction — which is usually faster, more private, and better for the genuinely good pieces. If a traditional in-home sale is the right fit for a particular estate, we'll say so and point you to reputable companies that run them.

Can you work with our attorney or realtor directly?

Gladly. Many of our estate clients come through Naples attorneys and realtors, and we're used to providing the written documentation an estate file or a listing needs.
