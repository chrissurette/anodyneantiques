Art · Naples, Florida

Paintings, gilt, and the odd looking glass.

Antique oils and portraits, watercolors and prints, period frames and mirrors — hung salon-style in a shop that happens to live inside an art gallery.

Visit the shop Sell a painting

What you'll find on the walls

  • Oil paintings — portraits, landscapes, still lifes, marine and sporting pictures, mostly 18th to early 20th century, European and American.
  • Works on paper — watercolors, drawings, engravings and hand-colored prints, botanical and natural-history plates.
  • Period gilt frames — including empty ones, which designers snap up faster than the pictures.
  • Mirrors — gilt, painted, Venetian, trumeau and the plain old looking glass with a good bevel.

We buy pictures for the picture, not the name — a good anonymous portrait with presence will always find a wall here. When something arrives with a signature and a story worth chasing, we chase it, and if it belongs in a bigger room we say so. How we place paintings at auction.

Buying and consigning art

If you have paintings, prints, frames or mirrors to sell, bring them in or send photographs — the front, the signature, and the back with its labels and inscriptions. We'll tell you what you have and what it's worth, free of charge, and offer to buy it, consign it, or send it to a specialist sale. More on evaluations.

The gallery we live in

Anodyne Antiques shares its rooms with Sharon Lynch Collections, a gallery of original floral paintings by the artist Sharon Lynch — peonies, tulips and hydrangeas on cobalt and turquoise, shown alongside the blue-and-white porcelain and Delft that inspire them. The pairing of 18th-century pictures and 21st-century canvases is the point of the place, and it makes for a very good hour on Shirley Street.

Sharon's originals are her own; enquiries about her work go to her gallery directly. Her paintings also become fabrics and pillows, a line of candles, and the hand-painted faux florals in the boutique.

Questions people ask

Do you buy paintings without a signature?

Yes. Quality, subject, period and condition matter more to us than a name. A fine anonymous portrait or landscape in a good frame is exactly what our designer clients are looking for.

Can you tell me if my painting is valuable?

Send photographs of the front, the signature and the back. We'll give you an honest opinion at no charge, and if it merits further research or a specialist sale we'll say so.

Do you sell empty frames?

Whenever we have them. Period gilt frames are among the first things to leave the shop.

Do you offer framing or restoration?

We don't do the work in-house, but we can recommend conservators and framers in Naples we trust with our own stock.

Always acquiring

Have something with a past?

We buy single pieces and entire estates — antiques, silver, art, and furniture. Honest evaluations, free of charge and free of pressure.
