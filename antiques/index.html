The shop · 6240 Shirley St, Naples

A small shop with a long memory.

Furniture, sterling silver, art, porcelain, lighting and the occasional curiosity — chosen by hand, one at a time, in a boutique in Naples' Shirley Street design district.

Hours & directions Call (239) 404-8505

What we carry

No lots, no filler, and nothing bought by the container. Everything on the floor was chosen because it was good, and most of it came from homes in Naples and along the Gulf coast — which means the inventory changes constantly, and the best excuse to visit is that you never quite know what's arrived.

Furniture & case goods

Commodes, chests, tables, desks, chairs and mirrors, mostly 18th to early 20th century, with a weakness for painted and gilt Continental pieces and anything with honest wear.

Sterling & silver

Tea and coffee services, flatware, trays, candlesticks and hollowware — American, English and Continental — that still know how to shine.

Art, gilt & mirrors

Oil paintings, portraits, watercolors and prints in period frames; looking glasses that sometimes outshine what they reflect. More on art.

Porcelain & glass

Blue-and-white, Delft, faience, Chinese export, Continental and English porcelain, crystal and cut glass. Handled with two hands, please.

Lighting & curiosities

Lamps, sconces and chandeliers, clocks, bronzes, boxes, books and — always — the odd beautiful thing that refused to be categorized.

A boutique, not a mall

We are one shop, one point of view, in a shared space with two good neighbors: Sharon Lynch Collections, a gallery of original floral paintings, and our sister shop Naples Estate Jewelry. Three collections, one very good address — and a pleasant hour if you're furnishing a house, hunting for a gift, or just looking.

Designers, dealers and decorators are always welcome; ask about the trade. And between the antiques and the gallery there's the boutique — candles, pillows made from paintings, Delft by the shelf, gifts, and a shop that goes all in on the holidays.

Selling, too

Everything in the shop was bought from someone. If you have a piece — or a house — to sell, see what we buy, or our estate services for anything larger than a car load.

Questions people ask

Where exactly is the shop?

6240 Shirley Street, Suite 104, Naples, FL 34109 — in the Shirley Street design district off Pine Ridge Road, inside Sharon Lynch Collections. Look for the sherbet-orange building. Directions.

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday 11am–3pm, Saturday 11am–4pm, closed Sunday — and gladly by appointment outside those hours. Call ahead if you're making a special trip.

Do you offer a trade discount?

Yes, for designers, decorators and dealers. Just ask.

Can you hold or deliver a piece?

We can hold a piece briefly with a deposit, and arrange delivery in Naples and Southwest Florida — or insured shipping further afield.

Is the inventory online?

Not yet — it turns over too quickly. Call or email about a category you're hunting for and we'll tell you what's in, and keep an eye out.

Always acquiring

Have something with a past?

We buy single pieces and entire estates — antiques, silver, art, and furniture. Honest evaluations, free of charge and free of pressure.
