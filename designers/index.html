To the trade · Naples, Florida

For designers who need the one piece.

The commode that makes the entry, the pair of jars for the console, a fabric no one else in Naples has, florals that will look the same on the install photo and a year later. One address, three collections, a trade account, and people who'll go looking for what you can't find.

What the trade gets here

  • Inventory that turns over. Because most of what we stock comes out of Naples estates, the floor changes weekly — painted and gilt Continental pieces, small-scale case furniture, seating with good frames, mirrors, lamps, blue-and-white, silver, art. Worth a walk-through every couple of weeks in season.
  • Sourcing. Tell us the piece — a pair of something, a size, a period, a color — and we'll look for it through the estates we handle and the dealers we know. No fee to look.
  • Delft and blue-and-white in depth. Ginger jars, garnitures, cachepots, planters and pots, chargers, tulipières — antique and new — for the pair you need to match and the mantel that needs a story.
  • Custom fabrics and pillows from Sharon Lynch's paintings: yardage for a statement chair or a whole scheme, coordinated pillows, exclusive to the project if you'd like it to be. Details.
  • Faux florals, hand-painted, arranged for the install and unchanged on day 400 — for homes that sit empty half the year, for model units, for the room that never gets the light. Details.
  • Client gifts. Sharon's candles, with their fragrance notes on the label, and small blue-and-white for closings and installs; standing orders so there's always a box ready. The candles.
  • Delivery to the project across Naples and Southwest Florida, including the buildings with elevator reservations and gate lists — we know the drill.
  • Trade pricing for designers, decorators and dealers with an account. Ask; it takes a minute to set up.

How it works

  1. Come in, or send us the project — photographs, the palette, the room, what's missing.
  2. We pull what we have, look for what we don't, and send photographs and dimensions.
  3. Approve, and we hold, deliver or ship to the install date.
  4. For custom textiles and florals, we confirm lead times up front and meet them.

The other half of what we do

Designers are often the first call when a client's parent dies, or when a couple downsizes and the good furniture has nowhere to go. We handle that too: a free walk-through, a written evaluation of the contents, and buyout, consignment or auction placement for what your client is letting go — so you can hand them one phone number instead of a problem. Trade questions

How do I set up a trade account?

Call or email with your firm's details; it takes a minute. Trade pricing applies to designers, decorators and dealers.

Can you hold pieces for a client presentation?

Yes — briefly, with a deposit, and we'll send photographs and dimensions for the board.

Can a fabric be exclusive to my project?

Ask. Sharon's designs are printed to order, and exclusivity for a project can usually be arranged.

Do you deliver to gated communities and high-rises?

Yes. We handle gate registration, insurance certificates and elevator reservations — the same way we do for the estates we clear.

Will you look for something specific?

Yes, at no charge. Tell us what, and we'll keep an eye out through the estates we handle and the dealers we know.

Always acquiring

Have something with a past?

We buy single pieces and entire estates — antiques, silver, art, and furniture. Honest evaluations, free of charge and free of pressure.
