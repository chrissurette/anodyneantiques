Fabrics & pillows · Sharon Lynch Collections

Her paintings, on your sofa.

Sharon Lynch's floral canvases — peonies on cobalt, wildflowers on turquoise, the daisies, the roses — printed onto fabric and sewn into pillows. Finished pillows are in the shop; custom sizes and fabric by the yard are made to order, and everything ships.

From canvas to cloth

Every design starts as one of Sharon's paintings. The image is reproduced onto fabric — the whole composition, or a detail of it, a single peony or a corner of the garden — and made into a pillow or supplied as yardage for upholstery, drapery, a headboard, a bench. The colors are hers: the cobalts and turquoises she paints on, the pinks and corals of the flowers. On a chair they do what the paintings do on a wall.

In stock, and made to order

  • Finished pillows — a rotating selection in the shop, ready to take home.
  • Made to order — choose the painting, the crop, the size and the fabric, and we'll have it made. Pairs and sets, oversized floor cushions, lumbar pillows for a bench.
  • Fabric by the yard — for your upholsterer, drapery maker or headboard. Designers use it for a single statement chair or a whole scheme.
  • Remote orders — everything can be chosen from photographs, paid for remotely and shipped anywhere in the country.

Ask in the shop or by email for current sizes, fabrics and lead times; they vary with the piece.

How to order

  1. Pick a painting — in the shop, or from photographs we send you.
  2. Tell us the size, how many, and where it's going (a sofa, a bed, a chair to be upholstered).
  3. We confirm the fabric, the crop of the image and the lead time, and take a deposit.
  4. Collect in Naples, or we ship.

For designers

A fabric no one else has, from a Naples artist, in colors that were made for Naples rooms. Custom yardage for a project, coordinated pillows across a scheme, and a matching candle for the install. How we work with the trade.

The paintings themselves

If a pillow leads you to want the canvas, the originals hang all around the boutique; enquiries go to Sharon Lynch Collections directly.

Fabric & pillow questions

Can I order a pillow without coming in?

Yes. We'll send photographs of the designs, you choose the painting, size and quantity, and we make and ship it.

Can I get fabric by the yard for my upholsterer?

Yes — custom yardage is made to order from any of Sharon's designs. Tell us the quantity and the use and we'll confirm the fabric and lead time.

How long does a made-to-order piece take?

It depends on the piece and the fabric; ask when you order and we'll give you a date.

Are the finished pillows in the shop different from made-to-order?

Same designs, ready now. Made-to-order lets you choose size, crop and fabric, and make pairs or sets.

Can I buy the painting too?

The originals are Sharon's; enquiries about them go to her gallery. We're happy to introduce you.

Always acquiring

Have something with a past?

We buy single pieces and entire estates — antiques, silver, art, and furniture. Honest evaluations, free of charge and free of pressure.
