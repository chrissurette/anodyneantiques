Estate services by area

Each area has its own logistics — a bridge, a gate, a condo building with rules about elevators — and its own kind of estate. These pages cover what's different about settling a home in each place, and how we work around it.

We also regularly serve Fort Myers Beach, Golden Gate, Ave Maria, and the rest of Naples — Pine Ridge, Vanderbilt Beach, the Moorings, Coquina Sands, Aqualane Shores, Lely and beyond. If you're anywhere in Collier or Lee County, call; if you're further, ask.

How far, how fast

  • Naples — Pelican Bay, Park Shore, Old Naples, Port Royal: usually within a day or two.
  • Marco Island, Bonita Springs, Estero: within a few days.
  • Fort Myers, Cape Coral, Fort Myers Beach: within a week — sooner when there's a closing.

Every visit is free and carries no obligation. For a single piece that's easy to carry, the shop is open Monday through Saturday; for anything bigger — or an entire house — we come to you.

Out-of-state families

A good share of the estates we handle belong to families in the Northeast and Midwest who can't be here. We coordinate access with a neighbor, realtor, attorney or property manager, send photographs and a written summary, and carry the plan out with your approval by phone or email. You don't need to fly down to get it done properly.
