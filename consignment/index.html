How consignment works

  1. Show us the piece — in the shop, by photograph, or at the house.
  2. We agree a price and a split in writing, along with how long it stays on the floor and what happens if it doesn't sell.
  3. It goes on display at 6240 Shirley Street, alongside the rest of the collection, and is offered to the designers, collectors and regulars who shop with us.
  4. You're paid when it sells. No fees up front, no storage charges.

What consigns well

Sterling silver, small and medium furniture with good lines, paintings and prints in period frames, mirrors, lamps and lighting, porcelain and glass, decorative objects with presence. In a word: the things that a designer walks in looking for on a Tuesday afternoon.

Very large case pieces, anything in poor condition, and reproductions are harder to sell on consignment; we'll tell you if a piece is better sold outright or sent to auction.

Consign, sell, or auction?

  • Consign when you can wait, and the piece is the sort of thing our Naples buyers are looking for. Higher return, slower clock.
  • Sell to us when you want it done today, or when there's a deadline behind you. What we buy.
  • Auction when a piece is rare, signed or documented enough to draw collectors from further afield. How auction placement works.

We'll recommend one, and we'll tell you why. Sometimes an estate uses all three.

Questions people ask

What's the consignment split?

It depends on the piece, its value and how much work is involved in selling it. We'll quote a percentage in writing before you commit, and the number won't change afterwards.

How long does a piece stay on the floor?

We agree a term at the start — commonly a few months, with a review if it hasn't found a buyer. At that point we can adjust the price, extend, return it, or make you a direct offer.

Can you collect it from the house?

Yes, across Naples and Southwest Florida. For larger pieces we'll arrange proper handling.

Is my piece insured while it's with you?

Consigned pieces are covered while in the shop; the details are in the consignment agreement you'll sign before anything is left with us.

How and when am I paid?

By check or transfer within an agreed number of days after the sale completes, with a statement showing the sale price and your share.
